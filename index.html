About

A Professional Advocate Ready To Assist You

Daisy Karimi Ndwiga is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya with a distinguished law career and wide experience in legal matters. She is also a Commissioner for Oaths and a Notary Public.

As litigation counsel she has handled numerous cases before Kenyan courts, from the subordinate courts to the courts of higher jurisdiction. She works fluently in English, Kiswahili and Embu, so she can serve clients from many backgrounds.
